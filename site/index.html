THE QUESTION THIS RESEARCH ASKS

Does looking up past failures improve an AI's troubleshooting?

Give the same new problem to the same model twice: once without retrieved incidents and once with relevant, sourced case summaries. Score both using the same rubric, then compare results. The test is designed, not executed.

WHY IT MATTERS

Better troubleshooting could mean shorter outages.

When authentication, messaging or infrastructure fails, people and businesses lose access to services they rely on. Learning from previous failures may help responders investigate more systematically. That benefit is a hypothesis here, not an outcome we have measured.
